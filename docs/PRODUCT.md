## Problem Being Addressed

Digital marketing teams often manage their workflow across many disconnected tools:

Typical workflow today:
- Idea generation $\rightarrow$ AI chat tools
- Script drafting $\rightarrow$ Google Docs
- Asset storage $\rightarrow$ Drive / Dropbox
- Client approvals $\rightarrow$ WhatsApp / Email
- Publishing $\rightarrow$ Instagram / TikTok apps
- Metrics $\rightarrow$ Platform dashboards

This fragmentation creates several problems:
- Time lost switching between tools
- Disorganized client feedback
- Poor version control of content
- No centralized analytics or learning loop
- Operational inefficiency when managing multiple clients

Hookra aims to **centralize and streamline this process inside one mobile-first system.**

## Core Product Concept

Hookra focuses on three major workflow stages:

### 1. Create
AI-assisted generation of structured social media content.

Example outputs:
- Hook
- Script
- Caption
- Call-to-action
- Hashtags

Goal: accelerate ideation and scripting.

### 2. Validate
Structured collaboration between agencies and clients.

Features include:
- Content drafts
- Client review
- Comment threads
- Approval / revision states

Goal: replace chaotic approval workflows.

### 3. Optimize
Analytics and insights that feed back into future content strategy.

Metrics could include:
- Views
- Engagement rate
- Comments
- Saves
- Performance comparisons

Goal: close the loop between **content creation and performance data.**

## Target User

**Sofía** — Social Media Manager at a small marketing agency

Sofía manages multiple client accounts and spends her day creating content, collecting approvals, and checking metrics across different tools. She's organized and strategy-minded, but frustrated by the operational overhead.

**Her core need:** A single system to create, approve, and measure content — so she can focus on strategy instead of switching between tools.

**Key pain points:**
- Copying content between multiple platforms
- Chasing client feedback through email and messaging apps
- Wasting time gathering metrics from different dashboards

**What matters to her:**
- Producing measurable results for clients
- Operating with clear, efficient processes
- Scaling the agency without adding chaos
